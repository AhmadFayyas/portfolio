Let me introduce myself

I'm a mobile engineer with 12 years on consumer products at scale. I'm currently on talabat's platform team, where the work is shared foundations rather than features: the design system every product team builds on, server-driven UI, and the component and API contracts other squads work against.

Before Flutter I spent nine years in native iOS, and I worked through the migration between them from both sides. That combination is the part I find most useful day to day: the framework is a choice rather than a ceiling, and when a problem needs platform channels, background location or a look at what Instruments is actually reporting, I can go there.

I write, too. 31.8k reputation on Stack Overflow across 678 answers, gold badges in Swift and iOS, and a Recognized Member of the Mobile Development Collective. Nine published technical articles on Flutter, Dart and Swift. Explaining things clearly turns out to be most of the job.
